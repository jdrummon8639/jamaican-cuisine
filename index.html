Welcome to Jamaican Cuisine

Jamaican food is known for bold flavors, rich spices, and comforting meals. This website highlights five of my favorite Jamaican dishes and gives visitors a quick introduction to each one.

Jamaican Oxtail with Rice and Peas

Oxtail is slowly cooked until tender in a rich, flavorful gravy. It is traditionally enjoyed with Jamaican rice and peas.

Common ingredients: oxtail, beans, rice, thyme, scallion, garlic, onion, peppers, and seasonings.

Jamaican Curry Chicken with White Rice

Jamaican curry chicken combines tender chicken with aromatic curry spices and vegetables, served with fluffy white rice.

Common ingredients: chicken, Jamaican curry powder, potato, onion, garlic, thyme, scallion, and peppers.

Jamaican Curry Goat with Rice and Peas

Curry goat is a flavorful Jamaican favorite made by slowly cooking seasoned goat meat in a rich curry sauce.

Common ingredients: goat meat, curry powder, potato, onion, garlic, thyme, scallion, and Scotch bonnet pepper.

Jamaican Brown Stew with White Rice

Brown stew chicken is cooked in a savory brown gravy with vegetables and Jamaican seasonings, making a hearty meal.

Common ingredients: chicken, onion, garlic, thyme, tomato, peppers, browning, and Jamaican seasonings.

Jamaican Patty

The Jamaican patty is a popular pastry filled with seasoned meat inside a golden, flaky crust. It is a favorite quick meal or snack.

Common ingredients: pastry dough, ground beef, onion, garlic, thyme, curry, peppers, and spices.

About This Website

The purpose of this website is to share knowledge about Jamaican food and introduce visitors to some popular dishes. The site is designed for food lovers, including people who already enjoy Jamaican cuisine and people who have never tried it. The website was created as a Capstone project using HTML and CSS.
